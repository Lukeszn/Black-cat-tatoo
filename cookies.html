Cookie policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This website doesn't use cookies. There's no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and nothing stored in your browser. The only exception is the optional Google map, which loads only if you click to show it.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can save in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), sites must ask your permission before using any that aren't strictly necessary.

Cookies we use

None. Our website doesn't set cookies, doesn't use local storage or session storage, and doesn't load third-party scripts. Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so visiting doesn't send your details to font providers.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Help pages: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.

Changes
