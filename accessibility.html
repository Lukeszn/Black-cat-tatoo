Accessibility statement

Last updated 23 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to find out about the studio and get in touch. This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and controls meet AA colour-contrast guidelines against the dark background.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Every tattoo photo has a written description for screen-reader users.
  • Form fields have labels, required fields are marked, and errors explain how to fix them.
  • The gallery viewer can be closed with Escape and browsed with the arrow keys or by swiping.
  • Animation is minimal and switches off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of content.

Known limitations

  • The optional Google map is provided by Google and may not be fully accessible. The address and a directions link are always shown as text beside it.
  • The headline font is decorative. Headings are short and repeated in plain text in page titles and navigation.

Visiting the studio

If you have access needs for your appointment — step-free access, extra breaks, a quieter time of day, or anything else — mention it in your enquiry or email lukeszns@gmail.com and we'll do what we can to help.

Feedback and alternative formats

If something on this site doesn't work for you, or you'd like information in another format, email lukeszns@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

This statement was prepared on 23 October 2026 based on a self-assessment using automated checks, keyboard-only testing and screen-reader spot checks.
