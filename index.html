Heal it as well as it was inked.

A fresh tattoo is a wound before it's art. How you look after it over the next few weeks decides how sharp it stays. You'll get this in person too — here it is whenever you need it.

  1. First few hours

    Leave the wrap on

    Keep the covering on for as long as you were told at your session. Remove it with clean hands, wash gently with lukewarm water and mild unscented soap, then pat dry.

  2. Days 1–3

    Wash and moisturise

    Wash gently twice a day and apply a thin layer of the recommended aftercare cream. Some redness, weeping and tenderness is normal.

  3. Weeks 1–2

    Peeling and itching

    Flaking and itching mean it's healing. Don't pick or scratch — let it shed on its own, keep moisturising lightly and wear loose clothing over it.

  4. Week 3 onwards

    Protect it for life

    Once fully healed, high-SPF sunscreen is your tattoo's best friend. Sun fades ink faster than anything else.

Do

  • Wash your hands before touching the tattoo
  • Use mild, fragrance-free soap and lukewarm water
  • Apply aftercare cream in thin layers
  • Wear loose, breathable clothing over it
  • Message the studio if you're unsure about anything

Don't

  • Soak it — no baths, pools or sea for 2–4 weeks
  • Expose fresh ink to direct sun or sunbeds
  • Pick, scratch or peel flaking skin
  • Use scented lotions, petroleum jelly or alcohol on it
  • Train hard on the area for the first few days

This is general guidance. Always follow the instructions you were given at your session. If you notice spreading redness, heat, swelling, pus or a fever, get medical advice promptly from a pharmacist, your GP or NHS 111.

Questions, answered.

It's my first tattoo — will that be okay?

Absolutely. Many of the studio's reviews come from first-timers. Everything gets explained before it happens, breaks are always fine, and no question is a silly one.

How do prices and deposits work?

Every tattoo is custom, so the price depends on size, detail and placement. You'll get a quote after your enquiry or consultation, and a deposit secures your date. The deposit goes towards the final price. See the booking terms for how deposits, rescheduling and cancellations work.

What do I need to bring?

Valid photo ID (passport, driving licence or a PASS-accredited card), something to eat and drink, and clothing that gives easy access to the placement. Please don't drink alcohol in the 24 hours before your session.

What's the age policy?

Strictly 18+, with valid photo ID — no exceptions, even with a parent's consent. Tattooing anyone under 18 is illegal in Northern Ireland.

Do you do cover-ups or reworks?

Case by case, since every existing tattoo is different. Send a clear, well-lit photo of the current piece with your enquiry so it can be assessed properly.

What happens to the details I send?

Your enquiry is emailed to the studio and used only to reply to you and arrange your tattoo. Nothing is sold or used for marketing, and this website sets no cookies. Read the full privacy notice.

Where exactly is the studio?

Inside 191 Hair and Beauty at 191 Union Street, Lurgan, BT66 8EQ. Directions are in the Find the studio section and come with your booking confirmation.
