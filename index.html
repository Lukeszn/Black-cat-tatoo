Aftercare

Heal it as well as it was inked.

Your tattoo is an open wound before it's art — how you treat it over the next few weeks decides how sharp it stays for decades. You'll get this run-through in person too, but here it is any time you need it.

🕐
First few hours

Leave the wrap on

Keep the covering on as advised at your session. When it's time, remove it with clean hands and gently wash the tattoo with lukewarm water and mild, unscented soap. Pat dry — never rub.

📅
Days 1–3

Wash & moisturise

Wash gently twice a day and apply a thin layer of the recommended aftercare cream. Thin is the key word — the skin needs to breathe. Some redness, oozing, and tenderness is completely normal.

🌒
Weeks 1–2

Peeling & itching

Flaking and itching means it's healing. Don't pick, scratch, or peel anything off — let it shed on its own. Keep moisturising lightly and wear loose, clean clothing over the area.

✨
Week 3 onwards

Protect it for life

Once fully healed, high-SPF sunscreen is your tattoo's best friend — sun fades ink faster than anything else. Spot anything that doesn't look right while healing? Get in touch, or see a pharmacist or GP.

✓  Do

  • Wash your hands before touching the tattoo
  • Use mild, fragrance-free soap and lukewarm water
  • Apply aftercare cream in thin layers
  • Wear loose, breathable clothing over it
  • Message the studio if you're unsure about anything

✕  Don't

  • Soak it — no baths, pools, or the sea for 2–4 weeks
  • Expose fresh ink to direct sun or sunbeds
  • Pick, scratch, or peel flaking skin
  • Use scented lotions, petroleum jelly, or alcohol on it
  • Do heavy gym sessions on the area in the first days

A note: this is general guidance — always follow the specific instructions given at your session, as they're tailored to your piece and skin. If you notice signs of infection such as spreading redness, heat, swelling, or fever, seek medical advice promptly.
